--- where the cross-core acquire load sits, computed ---
Index of the acquire load and of the first branch, counting
instructions from the start of each function:

  push_once              acquire at  5, first branch at  4, 28 instructions
  pop_once               acquire at  4, first branch at  3, 23 instructions
  push_once_uncached     acquire at  1, first branch at  4, 23 instructions
  pop_once_uncached      acquire at  1, first branch at  3, 18 instructions

In the cached arm the acquire load is reached only after a
branch: the producer consults its cached copy of the consumer
index first and re-reads the shared one only when that copy
says the queue is full. In the uncached arm the acquire load
precedes every branch, so it is paid on every call. This is
the mechanism behind A4b, shown rather than inferred from the
timing ratio. It also confirms that the uncached path is the
shorter one: it skips the cache refresh and the recheck.

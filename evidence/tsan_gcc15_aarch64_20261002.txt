mutex_queue is the only suite that blocks in a condition_variable wait,
and from ee5d6a9 it forces one. Under GCC 15's ThreadSanitizer on this
guest that wait reaches the sanitizer's __sigsetjmp interceptor, which
branches into glibc with br x2; glibc's bti c landing pad accepts no br
except through x16 or x17, and the CPU raises SIGILL.
evidence/gcc_tsan_cv_wait_20260920.txt reproduces it in twenty lines of
standard library. At c3b948b the suite never forces a park, and since a
park dies the same way, none of these 20 runs parked by chance either.

Negative control, c1_relaxed_publication, in the same builds

Both exited 66 and printed to stdout:

  C1: no observable corruption detected in this finite native run.
  The implementation remains intentionally invalid C++.

No torn record appeared in either run; the evidence is the race report.
Each stderr holds 10 reports and ends "ThreadSanitizer: reported 10
warnings". Each is a read by thread T2 in try_pop against a previous
write of size 8 by thread T1 in try_push, at the same address, in the main
thread's stack, where the control declares its queue. The ten addresses
are consecutive 8-byte words, 80 bytes in all, which is sizeof(Record):
one report per word of one slot's record. Nine reads are of size 8. The
tenth, at offset 72, is of size 2: that word starts with symbol_id, a
uint16_t.

              read, try_pop     write, try_push   addresses
  c3b948b     hpp:130 (cpp:176) hpp:100 (cpp:159) 0xffffffffeb80-0xffffffffebc8
  e8cc389     hpp:172 (cpp:214) hpp:140 (cpp:197) 0xffffffffeb80-0xffffffffebc8

hpp is src/spsc_ring_buffer.hpp, cpp src/c1_relaxed_publication.cpp. The

4. DOES THE COMPRESSED TRACE STRESS THE QUEUE?

  Not in §6.5's specified analysis, and it is the section that
  should decide C. B2 asks whether queue depth survives real
  microbursts; a mean rate does not fill a ring, a burst does.

  Two constraints pull against each other:
    - the peak must be high enough to load a 16,384-slot ring
    - both arms must stay valid, and the tuned mutex baseline
      failed §6.4's producer-lag gate at 2.5M/s and above, so a
      peak far past ~1M/s buys a sweep with no comparison in it

TAIL STRUCTURE  (samples with latency >= 1000 ns)
==============================================================================

drain span   = span of dequeue timestamps across a cluster: how
               long the backlog took to drain once the consumer
               was running again. NOT the length of the stall.
stall length = worst latency inside the cluster, a lower bound on
               how long the CPU was away. Quote this one.

mutex  @  100,000 records/s   (run 20.00 s)
    messages disturbed    1,062,550   (53.1275% of 2,000,000)
    their latency        median    6.33us   p99   14.71us   max  238.83us
    (stall statistics omitted: at this density adjacency is
     guaranteed and clustering carries no information)
    => PERVASIVE. 53.1% of messages are
       affected, so this is a cost paid per message, not
       an occasional stall. Look at the wait policy.
